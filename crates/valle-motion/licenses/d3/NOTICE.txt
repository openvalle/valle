D3 attribution

src/compute/scale.rs adapts tick generation, nice bounds, and band-scale
semantics from d3-array and d3-scale, implemented in Rust with Valle's
shared deterministic math. The ISC notices are preserved alongside this file.

src/compute/pie.rs and src/compute/curve.rs adapt pie angle allocation and
monotone-X cubic generation from d3-shape. src/geometry.rs PathData::sector
uses a frozen verb table derived from d3-shape annular-sector geometry so
inner radius, corner radius, and sweep can animate without changing topology.

Sources:
https://github.com/d3/d3-array
https://github.com/d3/d3-scale
