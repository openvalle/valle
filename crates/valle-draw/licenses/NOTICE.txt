Drawing and shader attribution

src/draw.rs adapts rounded-sector geometry from d3-shape in Rust. Motion
animation sectors live in valle-motion PathData::sector with a frozen verb
table; they do not call these topology-switching Draw branches.
Source: https://github.com/d3/d3-shape
License: d3-shape-ISC.txt

The following SkSL transitions adapt GL Transitions GLSL sources:
  crosswarp: Eke Peter (upstream credit: Eke Péter)
  directionalwarp: pschroen
  dreamyzoom: Zeh Fernando
  flyeye, linearblur, ripple: gre
  simplezoom: 0gust1

Source: https://github.com/gl-transitions/gl-transitions
License: GL-Transitions-MIT.txt

Changes include SkSL sampling interfaces, fixed transition parameters, and
reduced sampling in dreamyzoom. Original author credits are retained above.
